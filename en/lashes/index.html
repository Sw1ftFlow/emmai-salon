03 / FAQ

Questions about
lashes & brows.

What is a Korean lash lift?

A Korean lash lift lifts and curls your own lashes from the root and is combined with tinting. The result is longer, darker and more defined lashes – with no extensions at all.

What is brow lamination?

Brow lamination shapes and sets your own brow hairs in the desired direction and is combined with tinting. Your brows look fuller and well-groomed without daily styling.

Can I combine a lash lift and brow lamination?

Yes, Emmai Salon offers a Korean combo where the lash lift and brow lamination are done in the same visit.

What do the lash extensions look like?

The lashes are designed for your eye shape and features using techniques from Asia. The goal is a soft, natural result – never heavy.
