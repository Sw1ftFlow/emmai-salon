04 / Healing day by day

No surprises.
Just beautiful lips.

Day 1–3

The colour is much darker than the final result and the lips may be slightly swollen. Completely normal.

Day 4–7

The lips peel and the colour may look uneven. Don't pick – let the skin shed at its own pace.

Week 2

The colour may seem to almost disappear. Don't panic – the pigment is settling under the new skin.

Week 4–6

The true colour "blooms". A touch up after 6–8 weeks gives a perfect result.

05 / FAQ

Everything you want to know
about Lip Blush.

What is Lip Blush?

Lip Blush is a cosmetic tattoo (permanent makeup) where pigment is placed in the lips to give natural colour, a more defined outline, better symmetry and a fuller look – without fillers. The result looks like naturally tinted lips, not lipstick.

Is Lip Blush the same as a lip tattoo?

Yes. Lip Blush is a type of lip tattoo (läpptatuering in Swedish), also called permanent lip makeup or cosmetic tattooing. Unlike a traditional tattoo, the pigment is placed more superficially and in soft, natural tones, so it looks like your own lip colour and fades gradually over time.

Does Lip Blush hurt?

Emmai Salon uses a gentle technique and numbing, so most clients describe the treatment as almost painless – more of a light tickle than pain.

How long does Lip Blush last?

Lip Blush usually lasts 1–3 years depending on skin type, lifestyle and sun exposure. A touch up after 6–8 weeks is recommended for an even, long-lasting result.

What is the healing process like?

For the first few days the colour is darker and the lips may be slightly swollen. Around day 4–7 the lips peel and the colour looks lighter, and after 4–6 weeks the final, natural colour has settled.

Is Lip Blush a good alternative to fillers?

Yes, if you want fresher colour, a clearer outline and a fuller look without injections. Lip Blush does not change the volume of the lips, but creates a natural-looking optical effect of fuller lips.
